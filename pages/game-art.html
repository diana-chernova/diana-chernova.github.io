Game Art

Forest Stories is an educational game on the theme of man and nature. The genre of the game is point & click, it is designed for children from 5 to 8 years old. The goal of the game is for children to get acquainted with different animals, plants, natural phenomena, etc., learning them in a fun way.
Gameplay
The game duration is approximately 4-5 hours. It is divided into 12 levels, each containing 2 mini-games with unique gameplay for more variety. After passing each level, the child is rewarded with a bonus that opens access to the test with a corresponding theme to consolidate the knowledge gained.
